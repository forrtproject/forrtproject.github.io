Wording and source differences

  1. Accessibility: the translation uses невротипичност, while the English definition says neurodivergence. Should this be changed? It has been left as supplied because it changes meaning.
  2. Authorship: should правото на преписване на заслуги read правото на приписване на заслуги? The supplied wording is retained pending confirmation.
  3. Equivalence Testing: the Bulgarian Doc's definition cites Schuirmann, 1972, but both its reference note and the master definition say 1987. The existing master value, 1987, is retained. Please reconcile the Doc.
  4. Names in the credits: the existing master mixes Cyrillic names with Vladislava Trashlieva and Antonia Krasteva. The Tenzing record uses Marie Dokovova, while term credits use Мария Доковова. Please confirm preferred display names. Existing credits have been preserved.
  5. Open Scholarship: the last sentence switches to Отворената наука (Open Science) and научни дейности (scientific activities), while the English entry describes Open Scholarship and academic activities. Please agree the intended Bulgarian wording. Only the typo органичава was corrected to ограничава.
  6. STRANGE: the title uses саморазмисъл (self-reflection), but the definition uses самоселекция (self-selection). Should the title use the latter? The title wording is retained pending agreement.
  7. Objectivity: does нематериалистични convey the intended meaning of the English value-free? Please agree a replacement if needed; it has not been changed automatically.
  8. MOOCs: should бази данни be набори от данни for downloadable data sets? Also, is the Latin acronym MOOK intentional, or should it be Cyrillic МООК? Both source choices are retained.
  9. Corrigendum: please check grammatical agreement around the plural коригенда. The review proposed feminine agreement, but the definition explicitly identifies this as a Latin plural. Only the spelling корегенда → коригенда was corrected.
  10. Anonymity: please check личните опит и мнения. The review suggested changing опит to опити, which may shift the sense from experience to attempts. This requires translator judgment and has been left unchanged.
  11. Single-blind peer review: the source reference is Sherpa Romeo, and several related terms concern embargoes, paywalls and repositories. Please confirm whether these fields were copied from another entry and should be replaced. The malformed link and spelling errors were corrected, but the supplied source and related-term choices are retained.

All 60 automated review comments, their decisions and the verified technical fixes are recorded in the review findings.

The subsequent corrections are listed in the review findings. Malformed links were repaired without changing their displayed wording; obsolete internal routes were updated to verified equivalents.
