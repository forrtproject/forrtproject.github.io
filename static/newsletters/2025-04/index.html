Newsletter Highlights

 
Get Paid to Co-Create Open-Access Educational Materials!

FORRT is launching a funded initiative to co-create open-access, inclusive teaching materials to make open science more inclusive and accessible beyond elite institutions and Global North/ WEIRD contexts.

Contribute a set of materials and earn €400. Each set should include:

  • A set of teaching slides with script and pedagogical notes, editable and aligned with FORRT's Clusters.

  • A set of structured lesson plans incorporating student activities, styled according to Pownall et al. (2024).

  • All materials are designed to follow Bloom’s Taxonomy and are openly shared under a CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license.

Contributors will be credited on FORRT’s Contributors page and directly on the materials, and may be invited to co-author resulting publications (provided authorship criteria are met). Want to find out more? Please read here for additional information for contributors.

Join us in democratizing open science education!

Project Updates

New from FORRT: Revolutionizing Educational Research!

Introducing ELD CAMA — the Evidence in Learning and Didactics Community-Augmented Meta-analysis platform that transforms how we synthesize and share educational research. Unlike traditional reviews, ELD CAMA lets users upload individual studies, enabling real-time, open, and collaborative evidence synthesis.

Join a living, growing ecosystem of open educational science. Explore the resource here.

 

Call for Research Assistants: Make Replications Count

We are looking for multiple research assistants (6–10h/week, 12 months) for a paid position starting June 2025 - offering flexible working times and location. Must be enrolled at a German university due to grant requirements (but can be from any nationality and background)!

  • Code software tools to alert researchers about relevant replication studies.
  • Coordinate multiple international projects such as coding hackathons. 
  • Organize and accompany qualitative interviews.
