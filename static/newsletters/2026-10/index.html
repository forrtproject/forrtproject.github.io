Project Updates

Help Shape a New Tool for Discovering Research
Ever wondered whether a paper has been replicated, discussed on PubPeer, or retracted? 
FORRT ORE (Open Research Extension) brings this information directly into your academic search results, helping you explore the evidence behind the research you read. Available as a free, open-source alpha extension for Chrome and Edge, ORE highlights replications, PubPeer discussions, retractions, and links to free full texts.

🚀 Want to help improve ORE? 
We’re inviting researchers and curious users to test the tool, share feedback, and help make it more useful across disciplines. Expect some rough edges — your input matters!
👉 Install ORE from the Chrome Web Store and “Report an issue” to share feedback, or learn more.

Call for Papers

Neurodiversity and/as Interculturality  
📣 Language and Intercultural Communication invites abstract submissions for a special issue exploring how neurodiversity can inform intercultural communication and advance communicative and epistemic justice. The issue welcomes contributions examining neurodiversity as a dimension of difference, including its intersections with culture, communication, embodiment, decolonisation, and inclusive research practices.

📅 Abstract deadline: 31 December 2026
📝 Abstract length: 200–250 words
📧 Submit to: ListeningBeyondWords@leeds.ac.uk

🔎 Accepted authors will be notified by 31 January 2027, with the special issue scheduled for publication in October 2028. Researchers working across neurodiversity, intercultural communication, and related fields are encouraged to contribute!
