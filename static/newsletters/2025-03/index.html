Project Updates

New FORRT publication in the Royal Society Open Science

Several FORRT members have co-authored a new paper, published in the Royal Society Open Science! Led by Magda Skubera from Aston University, this systematic mapping review explores international initiatives aimed at increasing awareness and adoption of open research practices in psychology.

The study identifies 187 procedural, structural, and community-based initiatives, highlighting the rapid progress of open research and the collaborative efforts of individuals, institutions, publishers, and funders in reforming research culture. Well done team! Read the full paper here.

Guest blog on the Replication Network

The Replication Hub team has contributed a guest blog to the Replication Network, which aims to further the practice of replication in economics. Discussed were the upcoming Replication Research Symposium (May 14–16, 2025) and the launch of Replication Research, a diamond open-access journal, scheduled for late 2025. Read the blog here.

Events and Meetings


Join the FORRT Summaries hackathon – April 8th!
We would like to invite you to join us for the upcoming FORRT Summaries Hackathon on 8th of April (Online, 4-6pm CEST / 2-4pm UTC)! This event will focus on the FORRT Summaries project. Help summarize key papers in open science and support educators who may have limited time or access. No experience needed—just join, learn, and contribute! Co-leads will guide you throughout. Sign up by April 4th.

Contributors will be acknowledged on the website and those who fulfil the requirements (i.e., reviewing 10 summaries) will be invited to co-author any resulting manuscripts.
 
Help shape new replication discovery tools!
We’re inviting researchers and educators to join us for two informal, interactive focus groups where we’ll explore two new tools developed as part of the FORRT Replication Database (FReD) project. This is a chance to get a sneak peek, test the features, and share your ideas for future improvements.
 

FReD Annotator Focus Group

📅 Date: Thursday, April 10
🕞 Time: 3:30–5:00pm CEST
📍 Location: Online

FReD Annotator helps users identify replication attempts in their own reading lists, providing summaries of outcomes and links to relevant studies. It's designed to enhance the accessibility and usability of replication data for research and teaching.

Register here for FReD Annotator

 

FReD Explorer Focus Group

📅 Date: Friday, April 11
🕛 Time: 12:00–1:30pm CEST
📍 Location: Online

FReD Explorer is an interactive tool designed to help users navigate and discover replication studies. You can filter by key criteria, spot trends and gaps in the field, and easily access key information on past replications.

Register here for FReD Explorer

Advancing Open Science Together
The Replication Research Symposium, organized by FORRT and the Münster Center for Open Science, will take place May 14–16, 2025. This dynamic event brings together researchers from across disciplines to explore the power of replications and reproductions in advancing science.

You can expect expert-led discussions on the latest in replication research, exciting opportunities to contribute to a groundbreaking open-access replication journal, and a Replication Showcase featuring talks and posters from 12+ fields, including neuroscience, economics, and animal behavior—all designed to advance open and reproducible science. Check out the program details.
