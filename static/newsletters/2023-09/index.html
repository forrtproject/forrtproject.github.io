FORRT Newsletter, September 2023

This is FORRT's 4th newsletter informing the larger community about our activities.
 


 
September 2023

Hello there,

We hope this email finds you well. It is the beginning of a new academic year and a good time to look back at all we have achieved together! With this newsletter, we hope to inform the larger community about FORRT's activities in the last months. This digest is intended to help you cut through the noise and stay involved. If you missed our last newsletter, you can find it here.  

We'd love to hear your feedback, email info@forrt.org if you have thoughts or questions.

We hope you had a lovely summer, a well-deserved break and a good start to the new academic year!  


Newsletter Highlights


SIPS Mission Award 2023

FORRT was one of the winners of the 2023 SIPS Mission Award!

The award recognises and aims to increase awareness of exemplary projects which further the SIPS mission to improve psychological science.


Dorothy Bishop Award 2023
 
In case you missed this very exciting news, FORRT's very own founder and director, Flavio Azevedo, was one of the three winners of the prestigious Dorothy Bishop Award 2023 for the FORRT initiative.

The award was accepted on behalf of the whole FORRT community. A true testament of our community's hard work and the amazing people behind it!

FORRT Hosts the OSKB

FORRT has joined forces with the Center for Open Science to host the Open Scholarship Knowledge Base (OSKB). This represents a significant step forward in our shared mission to use education to promote open and reproducible scholarship!

New Partnership with OLS

The amazing folks at OLS have partnered with FORRT to help fund our community building! We are also working together to analyse policy relating to data science, open scholarship, and widening participation.
 


Project Updates

 
FORRT's first grant
  • In July, Replications & Reversals received an amazing grant from the Open Science Fund of the Dutch Research Council (NWO).
  • The team will use the grant to keep building and expanding the database of replication effects across social sciences and to develop two interactive apps, which will help people to explore, teach and cite replicable research. Stay tuned for more updates on this project!

 
SIPS 2023 Commendations
  • Three FORRT projects received SIPS 2023 Commendations: (1) Replications & Reversals; (2) Team Neurodiversity; and (3) Summaries. 
  • SIPS Commendations recognise projects which have contributed significant efforts to the SIPS Mission, to improve and reform psychological science. Well done to the amazing teams behind these three FORRT projects!

 
New FORRT Publications!
      
Over the last months, FORRT's teams have published three new papers:
 
  • In July, FORRT published a Perspective piece in Communications Psychology describing how the credibility revolution has led to positive structural, procedural, and community changes in research. Well done to all authors on this fantastic paper!
 
  • In May, the Impact on Students Team published their critical review in Royal Society Open Science. This paper summarises the existing evidence base around student outcomes associated with teaching open and reproducible scholarship, and associated outcomes. Well done team!
 
  • In March, Team Neurodiversity published an opinion piece in the BPS Cognitive Psychology Bulletin which gives a brief explanation of participatory research methods and why they are important for promoting neurodiversity in academia. Check out the open access version. Well done everyone!


Events and Meetings

 
Event highlights
  • In July, the Center for Open Science (COS) hosted a FORRT webinar on Big-Team Science Applied to Open Educational Resources and Metascience. We presented several ongoing projects at FORRT such as Reversals, Landscape, Pedagogies, Glossary among others. The recording is available on YouTube.
 
  • In June, Team Replications & Reversals and Team Neurodiversity ran two very successful hackathons at SIPS 2023! Team Neurodiversity also contributed an excellent lightning talk.
 
  • In June, FORRT held a workshop at the Open Science Community Groningen's Summer Event, where we had many enlightening discussions with participants about open and reproducible scholarship.
 
  • In June, several FORRT members met in-person for the first time at a FORRT Strategic Summit. They discussed future plans for FORRT and its long-term sustainability. The summit was very productive and also a lot of fun. After all, it is always nice to meet in person the people you value working with!
 
  • In March, FORRT hosted several successful workshops at the Unconference on Open Scholarship Practices in Education Research.

 
Upcoming events
  • On September 15th, Team Neurodiversity will be presenting two posters at the Neurodiversity in Higher Education Conference (University of Bristol, UK).
