About the clusters

Teaching Open and Reproducible Science shouldn't require educators to spend months sifting through a decade of literature. FORRT simplifies this process by providing a curated, expert-backed framework. Developed by over 100 scholars, our taxonomy organizes open scholarship into {{ with hugo.Data.clusters_v4 }}{{ len .clusters }}{{ else }}11{{ end }} distinct clusters, offering a clear pathway for integrating these tenets into your teaching and mentoring, regardless of your field or level of expertise.
