 🤖  AI meets research  🤖 
FORRT AI in Metascience Online Conference  
How can AI and large language models (LLMs) help us understand and improve how science works, and how are they changing science itself?  We invite you to explore these questions at the FORRT AI in Metascience Online Conference, featuring 33 accepted submissions from people studying or building with AI.  

📅 28–30 September 2026 (online, inclusive of various time zones, free to attend)

What to expect?
 💻 Online & accessible
Join via Google Meet, with live captions available in every session. Registered participants will receive joining details the week before via email, and selected talks will later be available on the FORRT YouTube channel.  

Programme
🤖 Monday: AI across the research pipeline
🔬 Tuesday: AI, replications & reproductions
🛠️ Wednesday: Three hands-on workshops
The full programme drops in early September.

Get Involved

 Looking for Leads
 Open Research Across Disciplines
FORRT is now hosting Open Research Across Disciplines, a curated collection of open research resources originally developed by Emily Farran, Priya Silverstein, Camilla Gilmore, and colleagues, alongside the UK Reproducibility Network.

We're looking for discipline leads (individuals, teams, or organisations) to help review and update their field's page, either as a one-off contribution or an annual commitment. Contributors will be recognised on the project, with annual leads also listed on the FORRT and UKRN webpages.

📩 Interested? Contact Priya Silverstein via email or the FORRT Slack.
