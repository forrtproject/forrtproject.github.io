FORRT Newsletter, January 2023

This is FORRT's 3rd newsletter informing the larger community about our activities.
Hello there,

We hope this email finds you well. This is FORRT's third newsletter and the first one for the year 2023. With this newsletter, we hope to inform the larger community about our activities since we understand that many of you are extremely busy and may not be able to keep up with ongoing projects and conversations on Slack. This digest is intended to help you cut through the noise and stay involved. We intend to send one digest every couple months. If you missed our last newsletter, you can find it here.  

We'd love to hear your feedback, email info@forrt.org if you have thoughts or questions.

We hope you had happy holidays, a well-deserved break and a good start to 2023!  


Newsletter Highlight

 
The glossary project launched Phase 2 at the Big Team Science Conference last October! The goals for Phase 2 are: glossary will (a) improve existing terminologies; (b) more than double the number of defined entries; and (c) include translations in several languages to increase accessibility and inclusion. 

This means we need tons of old and new collaborators -
have a look at the landing page of phase 2!

Upcoming Meetings & Events


Replications & Reversals Hackathon: #team-reversals is doing a hackathon on Thursday 26th of January from 12.00-18.00 pm CET. After a short introduction session, there will be a hackathon for the rest of the day to jointly work on the resource. The focus of this hackathon will be on finishing as many entries as possible to move towards a solid version 1 for our planned output piece. You can register here to receive the Zoom link and a reminder before the event!

Regular meetings

  • The #team-outreach also has a jour fixe, it meets every other Tuesday, from 17:00 to 18:00 CET (16:00 UK, 12:00 AM EST).

Project Updates

  • Team Neurodiversity are really happy to announce that their piece "Navigating Academia as Neurodivergent Researchers: Promoting Neurodiversity Within Open Scholarship" was published in the Association for Psychological Science Observer. Check out the article here, spread the word and keep an eye out for upcoming publications! 
  • Team Literature Landscape's preregistration is now finalised, the protocol was updated and they're currently in the process of screening the base literature. Their core team is also expanding - four new project managers recently joined.
  • Team Glossary's phase 2 is now live and was launched at the Big Team Science Conference in October 2022. Check the project's page for more updates. Twelve teams of translators have gathered to translate the glossary into languages other than English.
  • Team Reversals & Replications just updated its resource list, which now counts a fantastic number of 360+ entries across several disciplines, 250+ of which are completed. Join their hackathon on January 26th to help finish more entries!
