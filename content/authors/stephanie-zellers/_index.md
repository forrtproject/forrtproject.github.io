Stephanie Zellers (she, her, hers; PhD in psychology awarded in 2022 from the University of Minnesota) is a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute for Molecular Medicine Finland.  Her research projects are based in leveraging natural experiments to evaluate the causal influence of environmental exposures on health and wellbeing. She also works in reproducible research, where she provides cross-cutting Equity Diversity and Inclusion support in the iRISE consortium. She works to ensure that diversity is considered in all consortium activities, including intervention trials, systematic reviews, internal communication, and research dissemination. In her free time, she organizes Blues Dance events, where she also has experience in designing and implementing community safety and access policies that align with the culture, history, and values of Blues Dance. She  also likes studying Finnish and Chinese languages and hanging out with her cat Viivi.
