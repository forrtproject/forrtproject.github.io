The interface provides historical indicator views, source references, reporting years and CSV downloads linked to a dated release. It can support an introductory exercise in checking indicator definitions, reproducing a comparison from downloaded data, distinguishing percentage-point change from relative change, and separating observation years from release dates. National averages do not describe household-level inequality or establish causality, and a source-provided world aggregate is not an unweighted country average. GDT is an exploration layer rather than the producer of the underlying statistics. Classroom effectiveness has not been evaluated.
