Open Science BadgingOpen science badging identifies articles that have engaged in practices that help science reporting be more transparent and reproducible, including preregistration of studies, open access to study materials, and open access to study data.Preregistration reduces analytic flexibility, so readers can be more confident that the analyses reported in the publication were planned, unless described as post hoc or exploratory, and that the authors did not make post hoc decisions about participant selection or other decisions that can affect the reproducibility of findings.Open materials help make attempts to replicate or reproduce results easier because the materials are publicly available and not contingent on negotiation with responsible authors.Open data go even further, providing the data, so that others can verify the analyses.",
