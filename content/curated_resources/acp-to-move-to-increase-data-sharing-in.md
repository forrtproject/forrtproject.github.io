    "abstract": "Applied Cognitive Psychology is taking a further step toward greater openness and transparency in scientific research, by requiring authors of submitted papers to formally declare the status of the data on which their research papers are based. Up until now, we have encouraged authors to share access with others in the cause of open research. From now on, we will operate an \u201cExpects Data\u201d policy that requires authors of empirical papers to confirm in a published statement whether or not their data can be made available to other researchers. This does not mean we will cease to publish papers where data, for various good reasons, have been withheld, but the statement will provide greater clarity on data status for interested readers. The new process will involve just one additional step in the submission process. Authors will simply be required to check from a list of template options what the status of their data is. All statements regarding the status of data will be published and available to all readers. This requirement does not apply to authors of articles currently in peer review or production for ACP. The additional work for authors should be minimal, but we hope that the new requirement will have a positive impact on the communities that we support and serve.",
