    "abstract": "Since 2020, the GO FAIR Foundation has developed the Three-Point FAIRificaiton Framework (3PFF) as a light-weight, systematic and rigorous approach to make data and metadata FAIR. The 3PFF and its associated workshops have been used by numerous and diverse domain-communities (currently updated list of workshops can be found at https://www.gofair.foundation/3pffevents). The GO FAIR Foundation's FAIR Capacity Building Programme provides professional and qualified training for data stewards who aspire to run qualified 3PFF workshops and use 3PFF methods in their daily work. The FAIR Capacity Building Programme is designed to save costs and increase independence by locally embedding FAIR skills in the organization.
