The badges accompany scientific publications to indicate that the underlying study has been conducted in accordance with open- science principles and that the content of the publication has been made available and certified in a persistent location. We recommend that authors follow Wiley's Data Citation policy (https://authorservices.wiley.com/author-resources/Journal-Authors/open-access/data-sharing-citation/data-citation-policy.html). The policy recommends the use of certified data repositories listed on the Registry of Research Data Repositories (https://www.re3data.org/).
