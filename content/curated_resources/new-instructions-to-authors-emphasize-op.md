    "abstract": "Who reads the Author guidelines? Probably the same people reading this editorial. At face value, Author Guidelines are the detailed guidelines for submitting a manuscript. If a manuscript submitted to the journal does not follow the guidelines, it will be returned for changes and resubmission. This lengthens the review process and creates extra work for all. More importantly, a journal\u2019s Author Guidelines reflect the values of the journal, including standards for relevancy, rigor, ethics, transparency, clarity, brevity, and consistency of research communications, as well as ideals for removing bias in the review process. For example, the guideline that no identifying information\u2014author names, research sites, project names, or clinical trials registration number\u2014be in the manuscript ensures the double-masked review that the journal uses. Double-masked peer review means that the identity of both the author and reviewer is hidden.
