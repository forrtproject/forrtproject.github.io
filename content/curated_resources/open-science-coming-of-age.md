Open science provides complete access to the raw data alongside open access to the journal article. It can also include documenting in open online notebooks the entire scientific process during experiments, including video and interactive media that would be impossible in the traditional paper format. Open science also promotes the idea of making data free to manipulate and reuse and free to access. Open science not only reduces the need for scientists to male the same scientific discoveries, but it also reduces the need for the duplication of experiments by allowing the reuse of data that has already been gathered.
