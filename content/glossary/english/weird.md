    "definition": "This acronym refers to Western, Educated, Industrialized, Rich and Democratic societies. Most research is conducted with relatively homogeneous samples from WEIRD societies, and by researchers from those societies. This limits the generalizability of a large number of research findings, particularly given that WEIRD people are often psychological outliers. It has been argued that “WEIRD psychology” started to evolve culturally as a result of societal changes and religious beliefs in the Middle Ages in Europe. Critics of this term suggest it presents a binary view of the global population and erases variation that exists both between and within societies, and that other aspects of diversity are not captured.",
